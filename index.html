Researcher's notes

PRE-SPECIFIED · VERSION 1.0.0

Study protocol

01

Research question

Do my fiancée and I experience more calm and fewer episodes of tension or anger during a period when we maintain the boundaries below?

02

Background and beliefs

Earlier experiences without touching or showing our bodies to one another seemed calmer. Our Islamic understanding also motivates the question. We are tracking lived experiences—not measuring spiritual consequences or judging either person.

03

Hypothesis

While these boundaries are maintained, there will be fewer disagreements and less tension and anger, alongside more calm.

04

The four boundaries

  1. Refrain from physical contact with one another.
  2. Keep bodies covered in each other's presence.
  3. Refrain from sexual flirting.
  4. Refrain from undressing in front of one another, even for nonsexual reasons.
05

Stopping rule

The observation ends when either participant reports crossing any boundary. Either participant may also stop or withdraw voluntarily at any time. No new diary or event entries are accepted afterward.

06

Research limitations

This is an informal, non-randomized observational study of one relationship. An earlier period was not systematically measured as a baseline. Self-reporting, outside circumstances, communication changes, and early stopping may affect what you observe. Any pattern is descriptive and cannot establish causation.

END OF OBSERVATION

Apply the stopping rule

A boundary crossing stops this study permanently. Neither person should be pressured to continue. The recorded data remain available for your private review.
